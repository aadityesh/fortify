# Changelog

All notable changes to Fortify: App Lock. Versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-31)

- Setup asks for your name, and Fortify greets you with it on the unlock
  screen and the app list. Change it in Settings → General.
- The app list groups apps by category: Browsers, Web Apps, Social &
  Messaging, Productivity and more. Right-click an app to move it to another
  category; click a heading to fold it away.
- Choose how the app list looks: list, cards or grid.
- Search also matches category names.
- The unlock screen said "AppLock"; it now says Fortify.
- The README uses the app icon as its logo.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-31)

First public release. Fortify is now free and open source.

- Lock any app, including web apps, behind Touch ID or a 4 or 6 digit PIN.
- Lock card from the notch; the screen is covered the moment a locked app
  opens, including in full screen and on other desktops.
- Relock when an app is minimized, hidden or closed, when the Mac sleeps, or
  when the screen locks. Full-screen apps stay unlocked for a set time after
  you switch away.
- Lock shortcut (⌃⌘L by default) locks the app you're in and takes you back to
  the previous one.
- Forgot PIN: reset it with your Mac password.
- Light and dark appearance follow the Mac, with an override in Settings.
- No network access at all.
